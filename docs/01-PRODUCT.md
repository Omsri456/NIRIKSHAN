# 01 — PRODUCT
## MPLADS AI-Powered Monitoring & Analytics Platform

**Status:** MVP Product Baseline

## Product Vision
Build an AI-powered MPLADS monitoring and decision-support platform that analyzes work, expenditure, payment and completion data to identify anomalies, irregularities, potential cost overruns, delays, potentially duplicate works and compliance indicators.

The platform prioritizes cases through explainable risk scores and alerts and supports timely human review.

## Core User Journey
Data → Ingestion → Validation → Unified Work Data → Analytics/AI → Risk Engine → Alerts → Dashboard → Evidence → Investigation → Outcome

## Primary Stakeholders
- Ministry
- State Nodal Authority
- District Authority
- MP

Internal role:
- Admin

## Core MVP Features
1. Unified MPLADS data
2. Fund utilization analytics
3. Trend analytics
4. Cost anomaly detection
5. Timeline/delay detection
6. Payment anomaly detection
7. Similar-work NLP detection
8. Compliance indicators
9. Hybrid risk score
10. Risk-based alerts
11. Role-specific dashboards
12. Work intelligence page
13. Investigation workflow
14. Risk history

## Product Principle
The platform identifies works requiring attention. A risk score is not a finding of fraud or wrongdoing. Human authorities make the final determination.
