# 02.5 — PS TRACEABILITY
## SIH PS-102

### PS Objective
AI-powered monitoring and analytics for MPLADS to identify trends, anomalies, irregularities and potential fraud in fund utilization and project execution.

### Evaluation Alignment
The product is designed to address:
1. Novelty
2. Clarity
3. Feasibility
4. Practicability
5. Sustainability
6. Scale of impact
7. User experience
8. Project implementation

### Important Scope Boundary
Current datasets do not provide enough evidence for physical asset verification or a reliable supervised fraud classifier. These remain future enhancements.
